General Enquiries Wholesale Distribution

Your perfect partner for Wholesale and Distribution

Established in 1978, Lasgo Worldwide Media has become the UK’s longest established international wholesaler and distributor of home entertainment product. In doing so, Lasgo have created a one-stop solution for their customers’ needs, delivering service excellence to the home entertainment and non-traditional markets. From our humble beginnings in London’s Oxford Street, our success has been achieved through the company’s ability to diversify and identify new opportunities.

The journey to our current position of strength today as part of the multinational Little Group is based on the flexibility to be able to adapt to market conditions. For example, the industry has seen a resurgence of the Vinyl market (a market which Lasgo never left). As such we were well placed to take full advantage of this growth. We have also invested in the expansion of our Visual Entertainment and Merchandise divisions to meet a growing demand for fan-based physical products.

Lasgo Chrysalis Ltd is honoured to be given the prestigious Queen's Awards for Enterprise for International Trade 2020, Lasgo has been recognised for its excellence in International sales. Lasgo has twice before won The Queen’s Award for Export and sells the UK’s largest range of CD, DVD, Vinyl, a range of consumer electronics and merchandise associated with the film, music and games worlds to trade customers around the world.

Our Product Range

At Lasgo, we take pride in offering an extensive range of entertainment products to meet the diverse needs of our clients. Our inventory includes:

CDs

We provide a vast collection of music CDs, spanning various genres and artists, ensuring that your customers have access to the latest releases as well as timeless classics.

Vinyl

As enthusiasts of the vinyl resurgence, we offer a wide selection of vinyl records, catering to the growing demand for this nostalgic format.

DVD & Blu-ray

From blockbuster movies to critically acclaimed TV series, our comprehensive collection of DVDs and Blu-rays ensures that you can offer your customers the best in home entertainment.

Merchandise

Enhance your customers' fandom experience with our range of merchandise, including apparel, accessories, collectibles, and more. We work with licensed partners to bring you officially branded products that resonate with fans.

Consumer Electronics

Stay ahead in the rapidly evolving tech landscape with our selection of consumer electronics, including audio equipment, gaming accessories, and home entertainment systems. We carefully curate our offerings to provide the latest innovations and reliable brands.
